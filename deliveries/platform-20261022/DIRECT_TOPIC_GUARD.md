# Direct topic lookups must not use merely related evidence

A sources-only reproduction of a project-specific version-control question returned design-submission material. The question used a direct “what requirements are there” form, which the necessary-topic check did not recognize. Related drawing content could therefore become primary even though it did not support version-control requirements.

The shared topic check now also recognizes direct requirements, practices, contents, measures and rules lookups. It requires the complete requested topic in the existing evidence context. This does not hardcode a project or business answer, remove source/version protections, or change the independent coverage handling of parallel subquestions.

The original project document was audited read-only with OfficeCLI, native XML and nine visible embedded-media previews. Sufficient explicit version-control rules were not established for that source version. This does not prove the project did not manage versions, or that the whole library lacks relevant documents. Missing evidence remains a content boundary; it is not filled with another project's practice or invented rules. Embedded editable Visio hidden layers were not inspected.
